# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Konusbitr to be a project people are glad to spend their time on.
Everyone who takes part — contributors, reviewers, issue reporters, maintainers
— is entitled to a harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about trade-offs, not motives.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. "This query bypasses org scoping" is
  useful; "you clearly didn't read the spec" is not.
- Accept that maintainers say no. A rejected pull request is a scoping decision,
  not a verdict on you.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was once confused by the two-runtime
  boundary.
- Use the language of the repository — English — in issues, pull requests, and
  code comments, so everyone can follow along.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, or misrepresenting the licence or
  provenance of a contribution.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any chat or event that presents itself as a Konusbitr space —
and when you are representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately: open the
repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, which gives
you a private channel, or contact a maintainer directly through their listed
contact details. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers will respond in proportion to the behaviour, escalating as needed
from a private correction, to a warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban
from the project's spaces. Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith may
face the same consequences.
